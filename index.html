Haci Ismail Aslan

PhD Candidate & Research Assistant

I study how machine learning can make networked systems more reliable, secure, and efficient.

I am part of the Distributed and Operating Systems (DOS) group at TU Berlin, supervised by Prof. Odej Kao. My research connects robust graph learning and network intrusion detection with forecasting and resource management in the cloud-edge continuum.

01 Research

Learning for reliable systems.

Three connected areas shape my current work.

Network intrusion detection

I investigate graph neural networks for robust network intrusion detection, connecting learned representations with the security of networked systems.

Network securityGraph-based detection

Robust graph learning

I study how graph neural networks respond to changes in graph structure. In β-GNN, I proposed an ensemble approach to improve robustness and assess the severity of structural perturbations.

Graph neural networksRobustnessRepresentation learning

Intelligent cloud-edge orchestration

Within Swarmchestrate, I explore machine learning and time-series forecasting for proactive resource management across the cloud-edge continuum. My work focuses on QoS-aware scheduling and orchestration in distributed systems.

Time-series forecastingKubernetesQoS-aware scheduling
